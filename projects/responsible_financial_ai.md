# Proposed study: Responsible AI in Financial Decision-Making
**Status:** Proposal only; no analysis or study results claimed.

## Research question
How should managers balance financial outcomes, model calibration, and subgroup disparities in AI-supported decisions?

## Connection to experience and education
Builds on financial/client analytics and risk reporting, data-analytics methods, and MBA topics in governance, strategy, and managerial accountability.

## Proposed design
1. Use an appropriately licensed, public lending/credit dataset (verify terms and representativeness).
2. Document the decision setting and construct transparent baseline logistic regression plus tree-based models.
3. Evaluate discrimination (ROC-AUC), calibration, error rates by legally/ethically appropriate groups, and model drift.
4. Simulate threshold-based decisions under varying business costs, making clear that these are assumptions rather than observed profits.
5. Compare different fairness definitions and disclose incompatible trade-offs and data limitations.

## Research deliverables
- A reproducible notebook and data dictionary.
- A concise review of relevant economics, algorithmic fairness, and decision science literature.
- Sensitivity analyses, uncertainty estimates, and limitations.
- A 6–10 page working paper.

## Responsible-use caveats
This is educational research, not a credit decision system. Do not use private data or treat proxy group membership as a substitute for proper governance.
