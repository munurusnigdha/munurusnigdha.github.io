# Proposed study: Predictive Maintenance as a Management Decision
**Status:** Proposal only; no analysis or results claimed.

## Research question
Under which cost structures and failure patterns does predictive maintenance outperform scheduled maintenance or simple heuristic rules?

## Connection to experience and education
Connects prior predictive maintenance work at Cognizant with optimization, machine learning, MBA operations, and managerial resource allocation.

## Proposed design
1. Choose a publicly licensed equipment failure or sensor dataset and document provenance.
2. Establish a no-leakage, time-based train/test split and operational baseline rules.
3. Fit a transparent model and a nonlinear alternative, with calibrated predicted risks.
4. Specify scenario-based costs of missed failures, preventative service, and false alarms.
5. Compare policies on expected costs under assumptions, with uncertainty and sensitivity analysis.
6. Discuss the difference between retrospective model metrics and actual causal impact of deployment.

## Deliverables
- Reproducible notebook and evaluation plan.
- Policy cost curve and sensitivity plots.
- Research manuscript with limitations and avenues for field experiments.
